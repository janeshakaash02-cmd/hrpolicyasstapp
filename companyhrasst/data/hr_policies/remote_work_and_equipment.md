# Acme Corp - Remote Work & Equipment Policy

**Document ID:** HR-POL-005  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Version:** 2.1  

---

## 1. Remote & Hybrid Work Models
Acme Corp operates a flexible hybrid work model:
- **Hybrid Employees:** Expected to work at least **2 days per week** from the local office.
- **Fully Remote Employees:** Allowed for designated roles subject to VP approval and location compliance within approved tax jurisdictions.

---

## 2. Work From Home (WFH) Equipment Allowance
- New hires receive a one-time **$800 Home Office Setup Allowance** for purchasing ergonomic chairs, external monitors, keyboards, desks, and peripheral devices.
- Acme Corp provides a company laptop (MacBook Pro or Dell XPS) pre-configured with security software.

---

## 3. High-Speed Internet Stipend
- Remote and Hybrid employees receive a **$75 per month Internet & Cell Phone Reimbursement** towards high-speed home internet and mobile data.
- Expense claims must be submitted quarterly via ExpensePort.

---

## 4. Working Hours & Core Collaboration Hours
- Standard working week is **40 hours**.
- **Core Collaboration Hours:** All team members must be reachable on Slack and available for meetings between **10:00 AM and 3:00 PM (Local Time)** regardless of flexible start/end schedules.
