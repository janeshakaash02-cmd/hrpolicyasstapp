# Acme Corp - Code of Conduct & Workplace Ethics

**Document ID:** HR-POL-002  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Version:** 4.0  

---

## 1. Professional Ethics & Respect
Acme Corp is committed to maintaining a safe, inclusive, and professional environment free from harassment, discrimination, and intimidation.

- **Zero Tolerance Anti-Harassment:** Discrimination or harassment based on race, gender, sexual orientation, religion, disability, or age is strictly prohibited and results in immediate disciplinary action up to termination.
- **Reporting Violations:** Employees can report concerns anonymously via the Ethics Helpline (`ethics@acmecorp.com` or extension 4444). Whistleblower protection is strictly guaranteed.

---

## 2. Confidentiality & Data Privacy
- Employees must maintain strict confidentiality regarding company trade secrets, customer data, financial records, and unannounced product features.
- Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs) remain in effect during and after employment at Acme Corp.
- Company files must never be uploaded to unauthorized external personal cloud storage or unapproved third-party AI tools.

---

## 3. Social Media Guidelines
- Employees must clearly state that opinions expressed on personal social media accounts are personal and do not represent Acme Corp.
- Confidential business operations, customer names, or internal communications must never be posted on social platforms (LinkedIn, Twitter/X, Reddit, etc.).

---

## 4. Conflict of Interest
- Outside employment or freelance consulting that competes with Acme Corp or creates a clash of working hours requires prior written approval from the Department VP and HR.
- Acceptance of gifts from vendors, partners, or clients valued over **$100 USD** is prohibited without prior approval from Ethics Compliance.
