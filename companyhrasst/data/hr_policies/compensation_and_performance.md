# Acme Corp - Compensation & Performance Appraisal Policy

**Document ID:** HR-POL-004  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Version:** 3.0  

---

## 1. Salary Payment Schedule
- Salaries are paid on a **bi-weekly schedule** (every second Friday) via direct bank deposit.
- If a payday falls on a bank holiday, payment is disbursed on the preceding business day.

---

## 2. Performance Review Cycle
- **Mid-Year Check-in:** Conducted in July (Focus on goal tracking, feedback, and career growth alignment).
- **Annual Performance Review:** Conducted in December (Formal rating, merit salary increase evaluation, and annual bonus calculations).
- Ratings scale: 1 (Needs Improvement), 2 (Meets Expectations), 3 (Exceeds Expectations), 4 (Outstanding Performance).

---

## 3. Annual Merit & Bonus Structure
- Merit increases and performance bonuses are determined based on individual annual performance ratings and overall company financial performance.
- Eligible employees with a rating of "Exceeds Expectations" or "Outstanding" receive bonus payouts in February following the review cycle.

---

## 4. Promotion Policy
- Employees become eligible for promotion consideration after completing a minimum of **12 months in their current role** with a rating of "Exceeds Expectations" or higher.
