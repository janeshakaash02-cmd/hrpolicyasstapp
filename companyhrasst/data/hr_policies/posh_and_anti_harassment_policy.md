# Acme Corp - POSH (Prevention of Sexual Harassment) & Anti-Harassment Policy

**Document ID:** HR-POL-POSH-2026  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Applicability:** All Global Locations, Offices, Remote Workspaces, Business Trips & Digital Platforms  

---

## 1. Policy Statement & Objective
Acme Corp is committed to maintaining a safe, inclusive, and professional work environment free from sexual harassment, discrimination, bullying, and retaliation. Acme Corp enforces a **zero-tolerance policy** against sexual harassment under the Prevention of Sexual Harassment (POSH) framework.

Every employee, contractor, intern, and partner has the right to work in an environment free from unwelcome sexual behavior, inappropriate comments, physical advances, or hostile workplace conduct.

---

## 2. Scope & Applicability
This POSH policy applies to:
- **All Individuals:** Full-time, part-time, probationary, contract workers, consultants, interns, vendors, and job candidates.
- **All Locations:** Acme Corp physical offices, client locations, off-site retreats, business travel, company-sponsored social events, remote/work-from-home settings, and virtual collaboration channels (Slack, Zoom, Email).

---

## 3. Definition of Sexual Harassment
Sexual harassment includes any unwelcome act or behavior (whether directly or by implication) such as:
1. **Physical Conduct:** Unwelcome physical contact, touching, standing inappropriately close, or physical assault.
2. **Verbal Conduct:** Sexually suggestive comments, inappropriate jokes, repeated unwanted requests for dates, or intrusive inquiries into personal life.
3. **Non-Verbal / Visual Conduct:** Displaying sexually explicit posters, graphics, sending inappropriate emojis/GIFs, or staring/leering.
4. **Quid Pro Quo:** Demanding sexual favors in exchange for employment benefits (promotions, favorable performance ratings, hiring, or salary increases).
5. **Hostile Work Environment:** Creating an intimidating, hostile, humiliating, or offensive work environment based on sex/gender.

---

## 4. Internal Complaints Committee (ICC)
Acme Corp has constituted an independent **Internal Complaints Committee (ICC)** to address and investigate all POSH complaints with neutrality and sensitivity.

### Structure of the ICC:
- **Presiding Officer:** Senior female leader within Acme Corp.
- **Internal Members:** At least 2 employee representatives trained in HR and gender equality.
- **External Member:** Independent member from a non-governmental organization (NGO) or legal professional specializing in women's rights and POSH compliance.
- At least 50% of the committee members are women.

---

## 5. Complaint Filing Procedure
1. **Submission:** An aggrieved individual may submit a written complaint to the ICC via email at `posh@acmecorp.com` or `icc@acmecorp.com`, or via the anonymous Ethics Helpline within 3 months of the date of the incident.
2. **Conciliation (Optional):** Prior to initiating a formal inquiry, the complainant may request informal conciliation, provided no monetary settlement is made.
3. **Formal Inquiry:** If conciliation is not requested or fails, the ICC formally serves notice to the respondent and commences an investigation.

---

## 6. Investigation Timeline & Process
- **Acknowledgement:** Within **48 hours** of complaint submission.
- **Notice to Respondent:** Issued within **7 working days** with copy of complaint.
- **Inquiry Completion:** Concluded within **90 days** from date of complaint.
- **Findings & Report:** Issued within **10 days** of inquiry completion to management and involved parties.
- **Management Implementation:** Action taken within **60 days** of report receipt.

---

## 7. Interim Relief & Protections
During the inquiry, the ICC may recommend interim relief for the complainant:
- Transfer of the complainant or respondent to a different department/team.
- Granting paid administrative leave to the complainant for up to **3 months**.
- Restructuring reporting lines or project allocations to prevent interaction.

---

## 8. Confidentiality & Protection Against Retaliation
- **Strict Confidentiality:** Information regarding POSH complaints, identity of parties, and proceedings is strictly confidential under penalty of disciplinary action.
- **Anti-Retaliation Guarantee:** Acme Corp strictly prohibits retaliation against anyone filing a complaint or giving evidence. Retaliatory actions will lead to immediate employment termination.

---

## 9. Disciplinary Actions & Penalties
If allegations are substantiated following inquiry, penalties may include:
- Mandatory written apology & formal reprimand.
- Mandatory counseling or gender sensitivity re-training.
- Withholding of promotion, annual bonus, or pay increment.
- Demotion or suspension without pay.
- Immediate summary termination of employment without severance pay.
- Legal reporting to law enforcement authorities where applicable.

---

## 10. Mandatory POSH Training & Contact
- **New Hires:** Mandatory POSH compliance module within **30 days** of start date.
- **Annual Certification:** All employees must complete annual refresher training.
