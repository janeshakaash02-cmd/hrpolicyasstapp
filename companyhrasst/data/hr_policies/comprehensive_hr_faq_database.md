# SECTION 1: LEAVE, VACATION & TIME OFF POLICIES (Questions 1 to 70)

### Q1: What is the annual Paid Time Off (PTO) entitlement for full-time employees?
**Answer:** Full-time permanent employees at Acme Corp are entitled to **20 business days (160 hours)** of paid annual PTO per calendar year. PTO accrues at a rate of 1.67 days per fully worked calendar month.

### Q2: How does PTO accrue for part-time employees?
**Answer:** Part-time employees working at least 20 hours per week accrue PTO on a pro-rata basis proportional to their scheduled weekly hours relative to a 40-hour work week.

### Q3: What is the maximum number of unused PTO days that can be rolled over to the next year?
**Answer:** Employees may carry over a maximum of **5 unused PTO days** into the following calendar year. Carried-over days must be utilized before March 31st of the new calendar year, otherwise they expire without payout.

### Q4: How far in advance must PTO requests be submitted?
**Answer:** PTO requests for leaves lasting 3 consecutive days or more must be submitted through the HR Portal at least **5 business days in advance**. For leaves under 3 days, 48 hours' notice is requested except in emergencies.

### Q5: Can negative PTO balances be approved?
**Answer:** Under special circumstances, department managers and HR may approve an advance of up to **3 days of unaccrued PTO**. If an employee departs the company with a negative PTO balance, the cash equivalent will be deducted from their final paycheck where permitted by local law.

### Q6: How many paid sick days do employees receive annually?
**Answer:** All full-time employees receive **10 paid sick and wellness days** per year, credited upfront on January 1st of each calendar year.

### Q7: Is a medical certificate required for taking sick leave?
**Answer:** A medical certificate signed by a licensed healthcare provider is mandatory for sick leave exceeding **3 consecutive working days** or when sick leave is taken immediately before or after a paid company holiday.

### Q8: What is Acme Corp’s Paid Parental Leave policy for primary caregivers?
**Answer:** Primary caregivers (including birth mothers, fathers, or adoptive parents designated as primary care providers) receive **16 weeks of 100% fully paid parental leave**.

### Q9: What is the Paid Parental Leave policy for secondary caregivers?
**Answer:** Secondary caregivers receive **6 weeks of 100% fully paid parental leave** to support their growing family.

### Q10: What is the eligibility tenure requirement for Paid Parental Leave?
**Answer:** Employees must complete at least **6 months of continuous service** at Acme Corp prior to the expected date of birth or adoption placement to qualify for fully paid parental leave.

### Q11: Can parental leave be taken intermittently?
**Answer:** Yes, parental leave can be taken continuously or in minimum increments of 1-week blocks within the first 12 months following the child's birth or legal placement, subject to manager agreement.

### Q12: How many official public holidays does Acme Corp observe?
**Answer:** Acme Corp observes **10 official paid public holidays** annually, including New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day, Day after Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve, Christmas Day, and two regional holidays.

### Q13: What are Floating Holidays and how many do employees receive?
**Answer:** Employees receive **2 Floating Holidays** per year. Floating holidays allow employees to take time off for religious, cultural, or personal milestones not covered by official company holidays. Floating holidays do not carry over year-to-year.

### Q14: What is the Bereavement Leave policy for immediate family members?
**Answer:** Employees are eligible for up to **5 consecutive business days of 100% paid bereavement leave** for the loss of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, sibling, or legal guardian).

### Q15: What is the Bereavement Leave policy for extended family members?
**Answer:** Employees receive up to **2 consecutive business days of paid bereavement leave** for extended family members (grandparents, grandchildren, aunts, uncles, in-laws).

### Q16: Is Jury Duty leave paid at Acme Corp?
**Answer:** Yes, Acme Corp provides up to **10 days of fully paid Jury Duty leave**. Employees must provide a copy of the official court summons to HR upon receipt.

### Q17: What is the policy regarding Military Leave?
**Answer:** In accordance with federal guidelines, Acme Corp grants unpaid leave or differential pay (matching the gap between military pay and base salary) for up to **2 weeks of mandatory annual military reserve training**.

### Q18: What is Sabbatical Leave and who qualifies for it?
**Answer:** Employees with **5 years of continuous full-time service** at Acme Corp are eligible to apply for a **4-week fully paid Sabbatical Leave** for personal renewal, research, or volunteer service.

### Q19: Can PTO be cashed out during active employment?
**Answer:** No, active employees cannot cash out accrued PTO balances. PTO is intended for rest and recreation. Unused PTO is only paid out upon termination of employment where mandated by local labor laws.

### Q20: What happens if a public holiday falls on a weekend?
**Answer:** If a public holiday falls on a Saturday, it is observed on the preceding Friday. If it falls on a Sunday, it is observed on the following Monday.

# SECTION 2: HEALTH, BENEFITS & WELLNESS (Questions 71 to 140)

### Q71: What health insurance plans are offered by Acme Corp?
**Answer:** Acme Corp offers comprehensive Medical, Dental, and Vision insurance through the HealthPlan Platinum Network. Coverage includes PPO, High Deductible Health Plans (HDHP) with HSA, and HMO options.

### Q72: How much of the health insurance premium is paid by Acme Corp?
**Answer:** Acme Corp pays **100% of medical, dental, and vision premiums for full-time employees** and **75% of premiums for enrolled eligible dependents**.

### Q73: When does health insurance coverage become effective for new hires?
**Answer:** Health insurance coverage begins on the **first day of the month following the hire date**. (e.g., a hire date of Feb 15 means coverage starts March 1).

### Q74: What is the 401(k) retirement matching policy?
**Answer:** Acme Corp matches **100% of employee 401(k) contributions up to 5% of annual base salary**.

### Q75: What is the vesting schedule for 401(k) employer matching contributions?
**Answer:** Acme Corp provides **100% immediate vesting from Day 1** on all company matching 401(k) contributions.

### Q76: What is the monthly Wellness Allowance and what does it cover?
**Answer:** Employees receive a **$100 monthly Wellness Allowance**. It can be reimbursed for gym memberships, fitness classes, sports gear, wellness apps (Headspace, Calm), and massage therapy.

### Q77: What is the annual Professional Learning & Development Stipend?
**Answer:** All permanent full-time employees receive an annual **$1,500 Education & L&D Budget** for professional certifications, industry conferences, online learning (Coursera, Udemy), and technical books.

### Q78: Is Mental Health counseling available to employees?
**Answer:** Yes, Acme Corp provides a confidential Employee Assistance Program (EAP) covering up to **6 free therapy sessions per year** for employees and household family members.

### Q79: Does Acme Corp offer a Health Savings Account (HSA) contribution?
**Answer:** Yes, for employees enrolled in the HDHP plan, Acme Corp contributes **$500 annually for individuals** and **$1,000 annually for family plans** into their HSA account.

### Q80: Are dental and vision cleanings fully covered?
**Answer:** Under the HealthPlan Platinum Plan, preventative dental cleanings (twice per year) and annual comprehensive vision examinations are **100% covered with $0 copay**.

# SECTION 3: REMOTE WORK, HYBRID & STIPENDS (Questions 141 to 210)

### Q141: What is Acme Corp’s hybrid work policy?
**Answer:** Hybrid employees are required to work at least **2 days per week from an official Acme Corp office location**, with the remaining 3 days working remotely.

### Q142: What is the one-time Home Office Setup Allowance for remote hires?
**Answer:** New remote and hybrid hires receive a one-time **$800 Home Office Equipment Allowance** to purchase ergonomic desks, chairs, monitors, and accessories.

### Q143: What is the monthly Internet & Data Stipend amount?
**Answer:** Remote and hybrid employees receive a **$75 monthly Internet & Phone Stipend** to offset home broadband and mobile connectivity costs.

### Q144: What company equipment is provided to employees?
**Answer:** Every employee is issued a high-spec company laptop (MacBook Pro 16" or Dell XPS 15), encrypted security key, and required peripheral adapters.

### Q145: What are Core Collaboration Hours for remote employees?
**Answer:** All employees across flexible schedules must be online and available on Slack and Zoom during **Core Collaboration Hours from 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM (Local Time)**.

### Q146: How are travel expenses for mandatory team meetups reimbursed?
**Answer:** Mandatory business travel, flights, accommodation, and per-diem meals ($75/day) are 100% covered by Acme Corp via corporate card or ExpensePort reimbursement.

### Q147: What happens if company-issued hardware is damaged or lost?
**Answer:** Damaged or lost equipment must be reported to IT Security within **24 hours**. IT will dispatch a replacement device. Accidental first-time damage is fully covered by company insurance.

# SECTION 4: COMPENSATION, APPRAISALS & PROMOTIONS (Questions 211 to 280)

### Q211: What is the salary payment frequency?
**Answer:** Salaries are paid on a **bi-weekly schedule (26 pay periods per year)** every second Friday via direct bank deposit.

### Q212: When are annual performance reviews conducted?
**Answer:** Annual performance reviews take place in **December**, with ratings finalized in January and merit salary adjustments taking effect on February 1st.

### Q213: What is the eligibility criteria for promotion?
**Answer:** Employees are eligible for promotion consideration after completing at least **12 months in their current role** with an annual rating of "Exceeds Expectations" or higher.

### Q214: How are annual performance bonuses calculated?
**Answer:** Annual performance bonuses are calculated based on a weighted formula: **60% individual performance metrics and 40% overall company financial targets**.

### Q215: What is the rating scale used during performance appraisals?
**Answer:** Appraisals use a 4-point scale: **1 = Needs Improvement, 2 = Meets Expectations, 3 = Exceeds Expectations, 4 = Outstanding Contribution**.

# SECTION 5: ETHICS, CONDUCT & COMPLIANCE (Questions 281 to 350)

### Q281: What is Acme Corp’s policy on workplace harassment?
**Answer:** Acme Corp maintains a **zero-tolerance policy against all forms of harassment, discrimination, or bullying**. Any confirmed violation results in immediate employment termination.

### Q282: How can an employee report ethics or safety concerns anonymously?
**Answer:** Concerns can be reported 24/7 anonymously via the Ethics Helpline (`ethics@acmecorp.com`), internal portal, or toll-free hotline at 1-800-555-ACME.

### Q283: What is the policy on accepting gifts from vendors or clients?
**Answer:** Employees may not accept gifts, entertainment, or favors from vendors or clients with a value exceeding **$100 USD**. Any gift over $100 must be declined or disclosed to Compliance.

### Q284: Are employees allowed to engage in external freelance work?
**Answer:** Outside employment or freelancing requires prior written clearance from HR and Department VP to ensure there is no conflict of interest or work hour overlap.

### Q285: What is the notice period required for employee resignation?
**Answer:** Standard notice period is **2 weeks (10 business days)** for individual contributors and **4 weeks (20 business days)** for People Managers, Directors, and Executives.

# SECTION 6: POSH & ANTI-HARASSMENT COMPLIANCE (Questions 351 to 400)

### Q351: What is Acme Corp’s POSH (Prevention of Sexual Harassment) Policy?
**Answer:** Acme Corp enforces a strict zero-tolerance POSH (Prevention of Sexual Harassment) Policy to guarantee a safe, respectful, inclusive, and dignity-centered workplace for all individuals. Any form of sexual harassment—physical, verbal, visual, or digital—is strictly prohibited and subject to immediate disciplinary action up to termination of employment.

### Q352: What behaviors constitute sexual harassment under the POSH policy?
**Answer:** Sexual harassment includes any unwelcome sexual advances, requests for sexual favors, sexually suggestive comments, unwanted physical contact, displaying sexually explicit material, uninvited sexual messages/emails, or creating an intimidating, hostile, or offensive working environment.

### Q353: Who is covered and protected under the POSH policy?
**Answer:** The POSH policy protects all employees regardless of gender, employment type (full-time, part-time, temporary, contract), interns, job applicants, vendors, clients, and visitors across all Acme Corp offices, work-from-home locations, business trips, client sites, and digital communication platforms.

### Q354: What is the Internal Complaints Committee (ICC) and who are its members?
**Answer:** The Internal Complaints Committee (ICC) is an independent body dedicated to investigating POSH complaints. It comprises a senior female Presiding Officer, trained employee representatives, HR professionals, and an independent external member with expertise in sexual harassment law and gender rights.

### Q355: How can an employee submit a POSH complaint or report sexual harassment?
**Answer:** Employees can file a confidential POSH complaint by emailing `posh@acmecorp.com` or `icc@acmecorp.com`, submitting a report through the confidential Ethics Portal, or reaching out directly to any ICC Presiding Officer or HR Business Partner within 3 months of the incident.

### Q356: What is the timeline for investigating and resolving a POSH complaint?
**Answer:** The ICC acknowledges receipt of a complaint within 48 hours and initiates an inquiry within **7 working days**. The full investigation is completed within **90 days**, and a final report with recommendations is issued to management and both parties within **10 days** of inquiry completion.

### Q357: Are POSH complaints confidential and protected against retaliation?
**Answer:** Yes, strict 100% confidentiality is maintained throughout and after the inquiry. Retaliation or intimidation against any complainant, victim, or witness is a severe violation that results in immediate termination of the retaliating party.

### Q358: What interim relief options are available to a complainant during a POSH inquiry?
**Answer:** During an ongoing investigation, the complainant may request interim measures, including transfer of the complainant or respondent to another department, paid administrative leave for up to 3 months, or reassignment of reporting lines to prevent contact.

### Q359: What disciplinary actions are enforced if a POSH violation is proven?
**Answer:** Penalties for substantiated sexual harassment include mandatory written apology, formal reprimand, withholding of promotion/bonus, demotion, suspension without pay, or immediate summary dismissal/termination without severance, alongside potential legal reporting.

### Q360: Is POSH compliance training mandatory for all Acme Corp employees?
**Answer:** Yes, all new hires must complete mandatory POSH awareness training within **30 days of joining**. Annual refresher training is mandatory for all active employees and managers to maintain compliance certification.
