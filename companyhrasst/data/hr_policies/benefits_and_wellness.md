# Acme Corp - Health Benefits & Employee Wellness Policy

**Document ID:** HR-POL-003  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Version:** 2.5  

---

## 1. Health & Medical Insurance
- **Coverage:** Acme Corp covers **100% of health insurance premiums** for full-time employees and **75% of premiums** for eligible dependents (spouse and children).
- **Insurance Provider:** HealthPlan Platinum Network (Includes Medical, Dental, Vision, and Prescription Coverage).
- **Enrollment Period:** Open enrollment occurs annually from November 1 to November 30. New hires must enroll within **30 days of their start date**.

---

## 2. 401(k) Retirement & Financial Wellness
- **Employer Match:** Acme Corp matches **100% of employee contributions up to 5%** of base salary.
- **Vesting Schedule:** Employer matching contributions vest immediately (100% immediate vesting from Day 1).

---

## 3. Wellness Allowance & Fitness Stipend
- Employees receive a monthly **$100 Wellness Allowance** to spend on gym memberships, fitness classes, mental health apps (e.g., Headspace/Calm), or sports equipment.
- Claims must be submitted via ExpensePort before the 25th of each month with valid receipts.

---

## 4. Professional Learning & Development Stipend
- All permanent employees receive an annual **$1,500 Education Budget** for professional certifications, online courses (Coursera, Udemy, O'Reilly), technical books, and industry conference tickets.
- Approval requires pre-clearance from the employee's direct manager.
