# Acme Corp - Employee Leave & Time Off Policy

**Document ID:** HR-POL-001  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Version:** 3.2  

---

## 1. Overview
Acme Corp recognizes the importance of work-life balance and provides employees with generous paid and unpaid leave options. This policy applies to all full-time and part-time permanent employees.

---

## 2. Annual Paid Time Off (PTO)
- **Full-Time Employees:** Entitled to **20 business days** of paid annual leave per calendar year.
- **Accrual:** Leave accrues at a rate of 1.67 days per fully worked calendar month.
- **Carry-Over:** Employees may roll over a maximum of **5 unused PTO days** into the next calendar year. Carried-over days must be used by March 31st of the following year.
- **Requesting PTO:** Requests must be submitted via the HR Portal at least **5 business days in advance** for leaves exceeding 3 consecutive days.

---

## 3. Sick & Wellness Leave
- All full-time employees receive **10 paid sick/wellness days** annually granted on January 1st.
- Sick leave can be used for personal illness, medical appointments, or caring for immediate family members.
- A medical certificate from a licensed physician is required for sick leave exceeding **3 consecutive working days**.

---

## 4. Parental Leave
- **Primary Caregiver (Maternity/Paternity/Adoption):** Entitled to **16 weeks of fully paid parental leave**.
- **Secondary Caregiver:** Entitled to **6 weeks of fully paid parental leave**.
- **Eligibility:** Must have completed at least 6 months of continuous employment at Acme Corp prior to the expected date of birth or placement.

---

## 5. Public & Floating Holidays
- Acme Corp observes **10 official public holidays** each year.
- Additionally, employees receive **2 Floating Holidays** per year to use for religious, cultural, or personal celebrations of their choice.

---

## 6. Bereavement Leave
- Up to **5 consecutive paid working days** for the loss of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, sibling).
- Up to **2 consecutive paid working days** for extended family members (grandparents, in-laws).
